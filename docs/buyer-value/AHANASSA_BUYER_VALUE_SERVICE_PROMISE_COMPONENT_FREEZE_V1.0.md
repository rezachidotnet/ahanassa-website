## 1. Purpose

This document freezes the content, hierarchy, layout, visual treatment, responsive behavior, motion, localization, accessibility, and acceptance criteria of the Homepage Buyer Value / Service Promise component.

The component answers one buyer question:

> **Why should I place my steel request with Ahan Asa?**

It replaces the previous Homepage Evaluation / Assurance component. It does not explain the purchase process and does not repeat the Hero’s four-step micro-journey.

---

## 2. Journey position and ownership

Frozen position:

```text
Product Showcase
↓
Buyer Value / Service Promise
↓
Verified Evidence [conditional]
```

The component owns:

- human accountability;
- coordinated handling of diverse requested items;
- equal seriousness for small and bulk requests;
- follow-up through agreed delivery obligations.

It does not own:

- product discovery;
- price reporting;
- the detailed technical/commercial review process;
- operational statistics or evidence;
- industry case studies;
- the primary conversion CTA.

## 6. Claim-safety rules

The component MUST NOT claim or imply:

- the cheapest market price;
- the highest quality in the market;
- the fastest service;
- guaranteed supply or availability;
- guaranteed delivery time unless contractually agreed;
- unconditional transport inclusion;
- equal commercial terms for all order volumes;
- that submitting an RFQ creates a binding purchase.

The approved copy intentionally uses:

- `تعهدات توافق‌شده` instead of an unlimited “all commitments” claim;
- `در صورت نیاز و توافق` for loading and transport;
- quality of attention and review, not identical pricing or commercial conditions, for small and bulk requests.

Any future speed claim belongs to Verified Evidence and requires at least 100 eligible operational records plus the approved data-quality gates.

## 7. Content hierarchy

The rendered hierarchy is:

1. optional visual eyebrow;
2. H2;
3. one supporting statement;
4. four service promises;
5. no component-level CTA.

The H2 is the principal entry point. The eyebrow MUST remain secondary and MUST NOT compete with the H2.

Each promise contains:

- a small decorative number from `01` to `04`;
- one concise H3;
- one supporting paragraph.

The numbers communicate grouping and scanability, not a chronological process.

An unordered list is preferred because the four promises are not sequential steps. Decorative visible numbers MUST NOT cause duplicated or misleading screen-reader announcements.

## 9. Frozen visual direction

The component uses the Homepage **Content Section** archetype.

### 9.1 Surface

- Full-width Warm Cream section: `#FBF5EB`.
- Content aligns with the shared Homepage container.
- No large outer floating card.
- No engineering grid background.
- No Hero-style image panel.
- No section-level shadow.

The preceding Product Showcase SHOULD use a White or sufficiently distinct light surface so the transition into Buyer Value is visible without a heavy divider.

### 9.2 Header block

- Eyebrow: Forge Copper, small and restrained.
- H2: Steel Navy.
- Supporting statement: muted Navy-derived text.
- Text alignment follows locale direction: right for FA/AR, left for EN.
- Heading block has a controlled maximum line length.

### 9.3 Promise grid

- One flat 2×2 grid on desktop and tablet where copy fits.
- One column on mobile.
- Thin brand-derived dividers separate items.
- Promise items have no individual filled background by default.
- Promise items have no individual shadow.
- Promise items have no large independent radius.
- Numbers use Forge Copper as a small typographic marker.
- H3 uses Steel Navy.
- Body uses a readable muted Navy-derived color.
- Generic stock icons are prohibited.

### 9.4 Why 2×2 is frozen

Four columns are rejected for this version because the approved Persian, English, and Arabic copy requires comfortable line length. A 2×2 grid provides better multilingual resilience and avoids narrow, tall text columns.

## 10. Divider behavior

### Desktop / 2×2

- One vertical divider between columns.
- One horizontal divider between rows.
- RTL and LTR retain the same geometric structure.
- Internal padding creates separation; dividers do not touch the outer section edges.

### Mobile / 1-column

- Vertical divider is removed.
- A horizontal divider appears between adjacent promises.
- No divider appears after the final item.
- The first item does not receive an unnecessary top border.

Dividers MUST remain visible but quiet and MUST NOT use full-strength Forge Copper.

## 11. Spacing and dimensions

Target behavior:

| Element | Desktop | Mobile |
|---|---:|---:|
| Section vertical padding | `96–120px` | `56–72px` |
| Header to promise grid | `40–48px` | `28–36px` |
| Promise internal padding | `32–40px` | `24–28px 0` |
| Number to H3 | `12–16px` | `8–12px` |
| H3 to body | `10–14px` | `8–12px` |

Exact values MUST use the shared spacing scale. Implementations MUST NOT create one-off arbitrary spacing tokens solely for this component.

---

## 12. Typography behavior

- Estedad Variable remains the approved font across FA/EN/AR.
- FA and AR use genuine RTL layout; EN uses LTR.
- No artificial letter-spacing is permitted for Persian or Arabic.
- H2 uses responsive sizing and MUST remain subordinate to the Hero H1.
- H3 titles SHOULD normally remain within two lines at validated desktop widths.
- Body copy MUST remain readable and MUST NOT be compressed merely to equalize card height.
- Items in the same row MAY share equal visual height, but text MUST NOT be truncated.

---

## 13. Motion behavior

This is an explanatory component, not an interactive feature grid.

Approved entrance behavior:

- the section header may fade upward by no more than `12px`;
- the promise group may enter as one coherent unit;
- if item staggering is used, delay MUST NOT exceed `40–60ms` per item;
- total perceived entrance MUST remain below approximately `500ms`;
- animation runs once only.

Prohibited:

- each promise flying from a different direction;
- sequential process-like drawing between `01–04`;
- animated number counting;
- bouncing icons or markers;
- hover elevation on non-interactive promise items;
- text hidden until animation completes;
- layout shift during entrance.

The content MUST be visible by default. JavaScript or animation failure MUST NOT leave any item at `opacity: 0` or translated off-screen.

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` removes all non-essential entrance movement and stagger.

---

## 14. Interaction and CTA policy

The four promises are informational and MUST NOT appear clickable unless a later specification assigns a real destination to a specific item.

Therefore:

- no hover lift;
- no pointer cursor;
- no button-like border treatment;
- no hidden “learn more” interaction;
- no accordion behavior;
- no component-level primary CTA.

The Homepage’s conversion path remains owned by the Hero, Header, and Final CTA.

---

## 15. Responsive behavior

### Desktop

- Header content uses a controlled readable width.
- Promise grid is 2×2.
- Copy is not stretched across the entire viewport.
- All promise items align to the shared container.

### Tablet

- 2×2 is retained only while each column remains readable.
- The layout may switch to one column before the global mobile breakpoint if localization causes poor line length.
- No horizontal scrolling is permitted.

### Mobile

- Single-column promise list.
- Section remains full-width Warm Cream with standard mobile gutters.
- H2 and supporting text remain above the promise list.
- Dividers replace card containers.
- No essential copy is collapsed or hidden.

### Localization resilience

- Breakpoints MUST respond to content fit, not Persian copy alone.
- EN and AR MUST be visually validated rather than assumed to fit the FA layout.
- Directional spacing MUST use logical properties where practical.

---

## 16. Accessibility requirements

- Section uses a real H2 in the Homepage heading hierarchy.
- Promise titles use H3.
- Promises are represented as one semantic list.
- Decorative numbers are hidden from assistive technology when necessary to avoid duplication.
- Body text and headings meet applicable contrast requirements.
- Forge Copper is not the only means of grouping or distinction.
- Reading order matches visual order in FA, AR, and EN.
- No keyboard focus is introduced for non-interactive items.
- Zoom to 200% MUST not cause overlap, truncation, or horizontal page scrolling.
- The component remains complete with CSS motion disabled and JavaScript unavailable.

---

## 17. SEO and machine-readable content

- All four promises MUST be present as normal server-rendered text.
- The component MUST NOT hide alternate keyword-heavy copy for crawlers or agents.
- No unsupported Review, Rating, Product, Service, or Organization claims may be generated from these promises.
- The same primary meaning MUST be present on mobile and desktop.
- Localization MUST be human-readable and semantically equivalent; literal keyword repetition is prohibited.

---

## 18. Content governance

The four axes are promises about service behavior. Operational owners MUST ensure the website wording remains supportable.

Future changes require review when they:

- add absolute superiority claims;
- change the transport responsibility;
- imply a guaranteed response or delivery time;
- imply all volumes receive identical commercial terms;
- convert the component back into a process explanation;
- add a CTA competing with the primary RFQ path;
- change the component from flat promises to interactive cards.

Operational evidence MAY later strengthen these promises, but must be displayed in Verified Evidence rather than inserted as unqualified claims into this component.

---

## 19. Acceptance criteria

The component passes only when:

1. It appears after Product Showcase and before Verified Evidence when Evidence is eligible.
2. The canonical H2 is rendered correctly in all locales.
3. All four approved promises are present and in the frozen order.
4. No purchase-process steps are repeated.
5. No “cheapest,” “best,” “highest quality,” or “fastest” claim appears.
6. Loading and transport remain qualified by need and agreement.
7. Small and bulk requests are promised equal seriousness of attention, not identical commercial terms.
8. The section uses Warm Cream without a large outer card.
9. The promises use a flat 2×2 desktop grid and one-column mobile list.
10. Items are separated by quiet dividers rather than individual shadows.
11. Promise items do not appear clickable.
12. No generic stock icon is present.
13. Motion is restrained, one-time, and reduced-motion safe.
14. All content remains visible when JavaScript fails.
15. FA, EN, and AR layouts pass visual inspection.
16. Heading hierarchy, contrast, reading order, and 200% zoom pass accessibility checks.
17. The component introduces no independent primary CTA.

---

## 20. Required visual verification

Validate at minimum:

- FA desktop at a common laptop width;
- FA wide desktop;
- FA mobile;
- EN desktop and mobile;
- AR RTL representative desktop and mobile;
- 200% browser zoom;
- reduced-motion mode;
- JavaScript disabled or motion initialization failed;
- long-content stress test using the longest approved locale strings.

Reject the implementation if:

- it looks like four product cards;
- it looks like a chronological four-step process;
- text columns become narrow and excessively tall;
- Copper dominates the section;
- the section visually competes with Hero;
- any item becomes invisible due to motion failure;
- translation causes clipping or reordering.
